Managed IT, sized to fit.

We're not a big-box managed service provider (MSP) with one plan for everyone. We sit down with you, choose what we manage and how, and price it to fit.

Sound familiar?

One-size contracts

The big MSPs sell a package, and you pay for what you never use.

Tickets, not answers

Every call goes to a queue and a different person who has never seen your setup.

Too small for in-house IT

You need someone watching the systems, but not a full-time hire.

What you get.

  • Monitoring and patching for servers, network and devices
  • User onboarding and offboarding
  • Vendor and license management
  • Help when something breaks, from people who know your setup
  • A monthly review of what changed and what is next
  • A scope you can grow or shrink as you go
