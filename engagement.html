Common questions.

Do you only work in California?

No. Our headquarters is in Huntington Beach, and we work on-site across California and Arizona. We support clients remotely in all 50 states and travel on-site as required.

How big are the companies you work with?

Startups, small and growing companies are our sweet spot: usually businesses without a full IT department, or with one IT person who needs backup. Our team has been part of a few startups, so we know how to build IT that can grow without overspending early. We're a small team on purpose, and we keep our client list small enough to know every one of them.

Are you an MSP?

Not a big-box one. You can hire us for a single project, a managed IT plan built around what you actually need, or part-time IT leadership. Many clients start with a project and keep going from there.

Can you work alongside our IT person or current provider?

Yes. Co-managed IT is a common setup for us: your team keeps the day-to-day, and we take the projects, the security work or the parts nobody has time for.

Do you work with medical device and life sciences companies?

Yes. Our team has spent years running IT for medical device, diagnostics and certified lab companies, where access, change records and audit evidence have to hold up.

What happens if we stop working together?

You keep everything: passwords, admin access and documentation. We hand it over in writing, so the next person can pick it up on day one.
