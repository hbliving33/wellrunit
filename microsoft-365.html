Services / Microsoft 365

Your workplace, secured from the sign-in up.

Microsoft 365 starts with identity: who can sign in, from where, on what device. Then email, files and meetings. We set it up right, secure it, and keep licensing under control.

Sound familiar?

Nobody owns the tenant

Settings were clicked through years ago, admins come and go, and nobody is sure what is configured.

Licenses keep creeping up

You pay for seats and add-ons nobody uses, and every renewal costs more.

Files live everywhere

Shared drives, personal OneDrives and old file servers, with no clear owner or permissions.

What you get.

  • Identity first: Entra ID, phishing-resistant MFA, passkeys and conditional access
  • Tenant security review with a ranked fix list, tracked in Secure Score
  • Exchange Online, Teams, SharePoint and OneDrive migrations
  • SharePoint and OneDrive sharing and permissions cleaned up and kept that way
  • Devices enrolled and managed with Intune and Autopilot
  • Licensing review and cleanup
  • Standard onboarding and offboarding for every user
