Our story.

Thirty years of running IT, and one idea we kept coming back to.

Well-run IT is the IT nobody talks about. People sign in and get to work. The network stays up. Backups restore when you need them. The audit goes smoothly, and the person who left last Friday can't get back in.

That's the job, and it's the only promise we want to make. So it's our name.

Where we come from.

30

Years in IT leadership

Our team has spent three decades running IT operations: building and moving data centers and colocation facilities across the country, and running IT for medical device and diagnostics companies, manufacturers, startups and growing firms.

We've moved headquarters and data centers, built labs, and been on the IT side of SOX and ISO audits and HIPAA reviews. We handle networks, servers and cloud, data centers, Microsoft 365, business applications, cybersecurity, physical security and IT strategy.

Some of our longest client relationships began at OC TechWorks LLC, the Orange County IT firm our founder co-owned from start to finish. When it wound down, its clients came with us.

We started out as Cali IT Consulting. We're still headquartered in Huntington Beach, California, with remote offices in Arcata, California and Payson, Arizona. But our clients are across the country, and our name should say what we do, not where we are.
