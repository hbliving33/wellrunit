The 60-second IT health check.

Six honest questions. No email required, and your answers stay in your browser.

  1. Is multi-factor sign-in turned on for every account, including admins?

  2. Have you actually restored something from backup in the last six months?

  3. Do people who leave lose access to everything the same day?

  4. Are your firewall, servers and laptops patched every month?

  5. Do you know exactly which licenses you pay for, and who uses them?

  6. Is there a written plan for what happens if ransomware hits tomorrow?
