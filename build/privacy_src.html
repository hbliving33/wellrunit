Who we are

This website is operated by Well Run IT. This policy explains what information we collect through the site, how we use it and who we share it with.

Information we collect

When you contact us through Bernie, the assistant on this site, we collect what you enter: your name, company, email address, phone number, state, whether you're a new or existing customer, what you need help with, the callback times you pick, and the chat itself. For an emergency, we collect only your name and phone number.

Bernie never asks for passwords or account details, so please don't type them in. Bernie sends what you enter to our support mailbox as one email. It is not saved in a database on this website, and nothing from the chat is kept in your browser after you close the page.

Before Bernie can send anything, a Cloudflare Turnstile check confirms you're a person and not a bot. It usually happens without you doing anything.

If you mark a request as an emergency, Bernie asks only for your name and phone number, and sends them to our support mailbox marked urgent.

The IT health check on our About Us page runs entirely in your browser. Your answers are not sent to us unless you choose to send them through Bernie.

We count page visits without cookies. The count records only which page was viewed and the date. It does not record your IP address, your device or who you are.

Like any website, the services that host and deliver this site may receive standard technical information when a page loads, such as your IP address and browser type.

We do not use cookies, advertising trackers or third-party analytics on this site.

How we use it

We use the information you send us to reply to you and to provide the services you ask about. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers that run this site and deliver your message:

  • Cloudflare provides the domain name service and security for this website, runs Bernie, and runs the Turnstile bot check.
  • Microsoft 365 delivers Bernie's email to our support mailbox and stores it there.
  • Supabase stores the anonymous page counts.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.

The fonts on this site are served from this website itself, not from an outside font service. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Do Not Track

We do not track visitors over time or across other websites, so the site does not change its behavior in response to browser Do Not Track signals.

Reviewing or changing your information

To review, correct or delete information you've sent us, email support@wellrunit.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here and update the effective date at the top.
